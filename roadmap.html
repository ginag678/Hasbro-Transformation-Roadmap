Hasbro's CEO described 2025 as progress in the company's "evolution into a digital-first play and IP company" [19]. This roadmap sequences eight initiatives behind that shift, from the Blueprint 2.0 strategy in October 2022 through the Playing to Win plan that runs to 2027. Each initiative is placed by what it depends on, not by when Hasbro announced it. Two moves came before our window, Magic: The Gathering Arena and the D&D Beyond acquisition, and we include them because later work builds on them. The window covers about four and a half years, longer than a typical 18 to 36 month plan, because we are reconstructing a transformation already under way rather than planning a new one.

Why this order

Crawl

Three foundations had to come first. Magic: The Gathering Arena (PC 2019, mobile 2021) gave Wizards of the Coast a team that builds and runs a live digital game. Corporate process and systems modernization updated how IT, accounting, finance and HR work. Global supply chain transformation lowered logistics and production costs and introduced design-to-cost. Both operations workstreams also produced savings that Hasbro said it would reinvest in growth initiatives. Remove any one and something later loses its base: the digital experience Wizards needed to take on D&D Beyond and new games, the design method, or the processes AI runs on.

Walk

Each Walk initiative puts a Crawl capability to work in one limited area. The D&D Beyond acquisition extended Wizards' digital ecosystem to a single brand, and users grew more than 20% after the deal closed. Design acceleration applied design-to-cost, 3D printing and then AI to toy design, cutting the time from concept to physical prototype by roughly 80%. Self-published video games apply Wizards' in-house game experience to a first set of titles, starting with Exodus in April 2027. The first two have measured results that justify scaling in Run; the games still have to prove themselves.

Run

Run takes two Walk results to full scope, and each one needs organizational change. The D&D Beyond third-party marketplace opens a platform Hasbro first grew with its own D&D content to outside publishers, so Wizards has to work with those publishers as partners. Enterprise AI adoption carries AI from toy design into financial planning, forecasting, order management and supply chain operations. Hasbro reports the two together; treating design as the proving ground is our reading. The rollout relies on the back-office and supply chain work, and it changes how teams work: each chooses whether to use AI under company guidelines.

Gaps in the public record

  • Hasbro's 10-K and its December 2023 8-K do not say which corporate functions were outsourced or to which provider.
  • The start date of the multi-year supply chain initiative is not stated. Our Crawl window covers its first measured savings.
  • Only trade press confirms the D&D Beyond marketplace launch. Hasbro's Q4 2025 remarks mention D&D Beyond growth but not the marketplace.
  • The 80% design result comes from management remarks on an earnings call, not from a reported metric.
  • Hasbro's Q4 2024 remarks list AI under partnerships, while its 10-K and the Playing to Win release list it as transformation work. We follow the 10-K.
  • Hasbro has not published a sales target for Exodus.
  • Treating toy design as the proving ground for enterprise AI is our reading. Hasbro reports the two results together without linking them.
